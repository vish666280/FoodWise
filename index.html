STORAGE TIP

First in, first out.

Keep older food visible and place newer purchases behind it. This simple routine makes expiry tracking easier.

01 Check your fridge twice a week.
02 Label leftovers with the date.
03 Freeze food you won't use soon.
SURPLUS FOOD

Good food deserves a second chance.

When food is still safe to eat but your household won't use it, consider sharing it with someone who can.

1Check safety Only share food that is safe, fresh and properly stored.
2List surplus Add what you have, quantity and pickup information.
3Connect locally Coordinate with a trusted person or registered food-distribution organisation.

LEARN & ACT

Better habits start with better information.

🛒01

Plan before shopping

Check your fridge and make a realistic list based on meals you actually expect to cook.

📅02

Track expiry dates

Keep food visible, label leftovers and use items that need attention first.

🍲03

Love your leftovers

Turn extra portions into tomorrow's lunch, a new recipe or a freezer-ready meal.

♻️04

Dispose responsibly

Separate unavoidable food scraps and follow your local composting or organic-waste system.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Household action is part of the solution.

India's estimated household food waste is 55 kg per person per year, according to the UNEP Food Waste Index 2024 as cited by the Government of India. Digital tools can support better planning, storage, sharing and awareness.
